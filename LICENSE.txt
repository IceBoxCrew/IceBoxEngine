--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 A Key is required for the Editor Tools. The editor, the launcher and the
    updater require an activated License Key. Your Key states its activation
    policy, its edition, and its expiry date if it has one. The Runtime never
    requires a Key - see Section 9.6.

9.2 One computer at a time. Unless your Key states otherwise, a Key activates
    one computer at a time. Activating it on a second computer while it is
    bound to the first will be refused.

9.3 One online check, once. When you press Activate, the Software contacts our
    activation endpoint one time. That is the only moment it happens. After
    activation succeeds:

    * the Software never checks your license again - not at launch, not on a
      timer, not after an update, not ever;

    * the Software does not require an internet connection in order to run;
      and

    * if our activation service, our website or our studio should ever cease
      to exist, your activated installation keeps working.

    Separately from the license check, the updater asks our release manifest
    whether a newer version exists when it starts. That is a version check and
    not a license check, it says nothing about you, and you can switch it off
    with "Check for updates on startup" in the updater.

    What the activation request contains, what we store, on what legal basis
    and for how long, is set out in PRIVACY_NOTICE.txt, Section 9.

9.4 Reinstalling, and replacing your computer. Reactivating on the same
    computer - after reinstalling the operating system, for example - always
    works and costs you nothing. If you replace your computer, or your
    hardware changes enough that your Key no longer recognises the machine,
    write to us and we will move your Key to the new machine. We will not
    refuse a genuine request. We may refuse requests that are repeated in a
    pattern that is plainly not a machine replacement.

9.5 Keep your Key to yourself. Your Key is personal to you. Do not publish it,
    share it, resell it or post it anywhere.

9.6 Your Products never check anything. The licensing code is compiled into
    the editor, the launcher and the updater only. It is not part of IceBox
    Runtime or of the prebuilt core libraries. A Product you build therefore
    contains no license check, no key, no device identifier and no activation
    code, and never contacts us - not on first run, not ever. See
    PRIVACY_NOTICE.txt.

9.7 When we may revoke a Key. We may deactivate or revoke a Key only where:

    (a) the purchase was reversed, refunded or charged back, or was never
        paid for;

    (b) the Key was published, shared, resold, or used for more Seats or more

    (c) the Key was obtained fraudulently, or the activation mechanism was
        circumvented; or

    (d) this License has been terminated under Section 17.

    We will not revoke a Key for any other reason. Revoking a Key never
    affects Products you have already shipped or the people who received them
